How To Play

Easy to understand, challenging to master. Follow these three core steps to achieve enterprise-level high scores.

1 🧩

Inspect The Puzzle

Each round calls the Banana API on our secure server, providing an image-based equation with bananas substituting digits.

2 ⏱️

Beat The Clock

Calculate the unknown digit (0–9) before the timer runs out. Faster solutions award extra bonus points per second!

3 ❤️

Guard Your Lives

You start with 3 lives. Wrong solutions or clock expirations deduct a heart. Survive as long as you can!
